Common questions

How do I place an order?

Open the app, pick a restaurant from the home screen, add the dishes you want to your cart, then tap View Cart and Place Order. You'll be asked to sign in or create an account before the order is confirmed. Payment is collected when you pick the order up.

How do I create an account or sign in?

Tap Get Started on the welcome screen to create an account, or Sign In if you already have one. Signing up asks for your name, email and a password, then sends a 6-digit code to your email to confirm the address. One account works across every restaurant in the app.

I didn't get my verification code.

Codes arrive within a minute or two and expire after 15 minutes. Check your spam or junk folder first, then tap Resend code on the verification screen. If it still doesn't arrive, email us and we'll confirm your address manually.

I forgot my password.

Email support@monarllc.com from the address on your account and we'll send you a reset link.

Where do I track an order?

Open the Orders tab to see active and past orders. Tap any order to follow its status — received, preparing, ready for pickup, completed. Status updates arrive live while the screen is open.

How do I scan a QR code at my table?

Point your phone's camera at the QR code on your table. The link opens that restaurant's menu directly in the app. The app asks for camera permission only when you use the in-app scanner, and the camera is used for nothing else.

Can I cancel or change an order?

Contact the restaurant directly as soon as possible — once the kitchen has started preparing your order it can't be changed from the app. If you can't reach them, email us and we'll help.

How do I switch the app to Arabic?

Open the Profile tab and use the language selector under Preferences. The app fully supports right-to-left layout.

Your account and data

You can review or change the name, email and phone number on your account from the Profile tab in the app.

To delete your account and its data, email support@monarllc.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my account". We remove the account and its personal data within 30 days and confirm by email when it's done.

Reporting a problem

If something in the app isn't working, send us these details so we can reproduce it:

  1. What you were trying to do, and what happened instead
  2. The restaurant and order number, if the problem involves an order
  3. Your device and iOS version (for example, iPhone 15, iOS 18.4)
  4. A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one

For restaurant owners

Monar also provides a management dashboard for restaurants — menus, orders, tables and staff accounts. If you run a restaurant and need help with your dashboard or subscription, email support@monarllc.com and mention your restaurant's name.
